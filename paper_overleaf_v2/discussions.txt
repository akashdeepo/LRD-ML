AD: Hey Nicholas, worked through all 12 items from your checklist + ChatGPT review (new commit pushed as Phase 7; Overleaf bundle in paper_overleaf/ is current). Big flag: the layered ablation you asked for produced a more honest result than what we had before — Model C still beats HAR by 4.6–8.2% pooled, but most of that gain comes from VIX/MOVE alone (HAR-X = A1), and the persistence aggregates only add ~0.5 pp at h=1, h=5 pooled, with the real incremental gains showing up at h=22 (+1.8 pp beyond HAR-X) and in stress regimes (+1.9 pp at h=5 high-VIX, +1.7 pp at h=22 COVID), so the "persistence as system-wide state variable" headline isn't dead but it's smaller and more conditional than we were claiming. The save is the vol-managed portfolio (Moreira-Muir, item 11): C-managed Sharpe in COVID = 1.37 vs unmanaged 0.65, CER +12.3% vs −3.0%, with persistence aggregates paying an incremental Sharpe of ~0.31 over HAR-X-managed in stress — so economic significance compensates for the weaker statistical headline. Two other things worth flagging: I switched DM to HLN-corrected panel-aware (the old plain DM stats of 15–24 were inflated ~6× by ignoring within-date cross-sectional dependence; real HLN-DM is +3.15/+3.87/+2.33, still all significant), and the robustness Table 9 confirms +4–9% across the LW estimator, windows 500/1000, squared-returns target, and liquidity halves — nothing fragile.
